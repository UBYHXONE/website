The Microwave-CVD advantage.

An energy-efficient stainless-steel reactor system that replaces costly electrical-resistance furnaces, cracking polyolefin waste directly into Green Hydrogen and battery-grade MWCNTs.

Traditional vs. UBYH

A fundamentally more efficient way to crack hydrocarbons.

Conventional thermal processes heat the whole reactor mass through resistance elements. Microwave-CVD couples energy directly into the catalyst and feedstock.

Conventional

Electrical resistance furnaces

  • High capital and energy cost to heat the entire furnace mass
  • Slow ramp-up and poor thermal responsiveness
  • Indirect heating limits selectivity and yield control
  • Heavy footprint, difficult to distribute
UBYH Microwave-CVD

Stainless-steel MW-CVD reactor

  • Low CapEx: energy delivered directly where the reaction happens
  • Rapid, selective heating of catalyst and feedstock
  • Higher control over H₂ and MWCNT product quality
  • Compact, modular, deployable near waste sources

Reactor System

Built for efficiency, control, and scale.

Direct energy coupling

Microwaves deliver energy into the catalyst and feedstock rather than heating the full reactor mass, cutting energy demand.

Stainless-steel construction

A robust, corrosion-resistant reactor engineered for continuous industrial operation and straightforward maintenance.

Low CapEx & modular

A compact footprint lowers capital cost and enables deployment close to where plastic waste is generated.
