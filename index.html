Why UBYH

Two high-value outputs from a single waste stream.

One microwave-driven catalytic reaction cracks end-of-life plastics into clean fuel and advanced materials, closing the loop on polyolefin waste.

Green Hydrogen (H₂)

Clean hydrogen produced without steam methane reforming, ready for industrial fuel, fuel cells, and localized energy off-take.

Battery-Grade MWCNTs

High-purity multi-walled carbon nanotubes for Li-ion electrodes, conductive polymers, and structural composites.

Circular by Design

Every tonne of polyolefin feedstock is diverted from landfill and incineration, aligned with national green-energy missions.

The Process

Microwave Catalytic Pyrolysis, end to end.

A single, energy-efficient reaction path from mixed plastic waste to two market-ready products.

Feedstock
Waste Plastics
Post-consumer polyolefins (PE / PP) collected and shredded.
PEPP
Reaction
Microwave Catalytic Pyrolysis
Targeted microwave heating cracks hydrocarbons over a catalyst in a stainless-steel reactor.
Output
Green H₂ + MWCNTs
Clean hydrogen gas and battery-grade carbon nanotubes, separated and collected.
H₂MWCNT
